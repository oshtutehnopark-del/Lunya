# Pip episode plan

One new episode every 2 days. Take the first topic marked `[ ]`, make it, then mark it `[x]` with the date.

- [x] ep01-colors — Colors: red apple, blue ball, yellow sun, green leaf; count 3 apples (2026-11-19)
- [ ] ep02-shapes — Shapes: circle, square, triangle, star; count 4 stars
- [ ] ep03-animal-sounds — Farm animals and their sounds: cow, duck, sheep, cat
- [ ] ep04-numbers — Numbers 1 to 5 with balloons
- [ ] ep05-fruits — Fruits: banana, pear, orange, strawberry
- [ ] ep06-big-small — Big and small: a big ball and a small ball, a big tree and a small tree
- [ ] ep07-weather — Weather: sunny, rainy, windy, snowy
- [ ] ep08-rainbow — Rainbow colors in order
- [ ] ep09-body — Body parts: eyes, beak, wings, feet (Pip's own)
- [ ] ep10-transport — Car, bus, train, plane and their sounds
- [ ] ep11-up-down — Up and down, in and out
- [ ] ep12-bedtime — Bedtime: moon, stars, pillow, "good night"

When the list runs out, add 6 new toddler topics in the same style (one simple concept, 4 items, a short counting or game moment, a goodbye).
